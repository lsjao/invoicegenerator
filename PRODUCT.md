# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Existing codebase: static HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript, no build step. Published as a single-page app; data stays in the browser, encrypted.

## Users
One person who pays subcontractors for site labour. Works on a laptop, once a week, turning pasted attendance text (notes or messages saying who worked which property on which day) into one invoice per subcontractor, then checking weekly sheets, timesheets and totals. Occasional phone use for checking. Shares the Weekly Sheet with a boss or others.

## Product Purpose
Make weekly subcontractor labour invoices fast and consistent: paste attendance text, check the extracted days, queue and download the invoices, and keep every record (past records, properties, subcontractors) in one encrypted place. Success is a week's batch done in minutes with no numbering or date mistakes.

## Positioning
The invoices are issued by the payer on behalf of the subcontractors, from rough text, with sequential numbering, duplicate detection and a shareable weekly breakdown.

## Operating Context
Weekly batch from pasted text. Saved subcontractors and properties fill in rates and bank details. Invoice numbers run in one sequence. Past invoices are imported as records and never change numbering. The Weekly Sheet is sent to others.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Quick paste (by person or daily roster), form entry, past-record import, queue with PDF and ZIP download, subcontractors, properties, Weekly Sheet, Timesheet, Dashboard, encrypted setup export and import. Must keep working as static files. All features stay.

## Brand Commitments
Dark green and gold from the pound-coin logo. Inter typeface. The invoice PDF design (green header, gold rule) is fixed and must not change.

## Product Principles
- Paste first: the fastest path from messy text to correct invoices.
- Show what was understood: every guess (names, dates, years) is visible and correctable.
- Never lose or change data silently.
- One vocabulary for every screen.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Readable contrast, visible keyboard focus, usable at laptop widths and acceptable on a phone.
