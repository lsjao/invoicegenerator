# Design

Operate-mode tool for a weekly batch task. Restrained, quiet, fast to scan.

## World
A payer's ledger: warm paper page (#F4F3EE), white working surfaces, ink-green text (#16211A), one accent green (#1A3920) for primary actions, selection and state. Gold (#C8B21B) appears only in the coin logo, text selection and the invoice PDF.

## Type
Inter only. Scale 12 / 13.5 / 14 / 15 / 20. Tabular figures for every number that lines up. Uppercase 11px labels only for table headers.

## Layout
Create is one workspace: paste on the left with a ledger of extracted invoices under it (one row each: include, name, number and dates, days, total, check, actions), invoice preview on the right. A sticky action bar holds the primary action. Detail (paid, duplicates, warnings) opens inline under the row only when something needs attention.

## Components
Buttons: primary green, secondary white with hairline, ghost. Status tags: Ready (green tint), Check (amber tint), to fix (red tint). Hairline borders, radius 10, light shadows. Focus: 2px green ring. Dialogs and toasts use the same surfaces.

## Fixed
Invoice PDF design, green and gold brand, Inter.
